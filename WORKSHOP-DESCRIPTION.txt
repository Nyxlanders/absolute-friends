Absolute Friends: Refriended

A community edition of Absolute Friends by pjhgamma, packaged by Nyxlanders
with a new cover. Based on version 0.3.0; gameplay is unchanged in this edition.

Absolute Friends provides configurable friendship rules, friendly-fire
protection, friendship sharing between players, and tools for managing and
travelling with creature companions.

Includes all ten built-in modules: Core, Manager, Meadow, Visualizer, General,
Items, Players, Creatures, Iterators, and Pilgrimage.
Resonance, Friendship Editor, and Chained Together are separate addons and are
not bundled here.

Compatibility
This edition retains the original mod and plugin IDs for compatibility with
existing settings and addons. Enable only one edition of Absolute Friends.
The package targets Rain World v1.11.8.
